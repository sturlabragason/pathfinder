# Surface: Caladrel's sheet (site/index.html)

Mode: Operate (at the table, phone first); Read for the Lore drawer.
Audience/job: Sturla mid-session needs a number or a spell rule in one tap; between sessions he prepares spells.
Constraints: static, no build, offline-tolerant, localStorage only, noindex. Spell data comes from pathfinder_spells (spellbook.json), with a bundled copy.
Unresolved: level-7 feat and the two level-7 free spells are unrecorded; 4th-level slots: rules give 3 where the earlier sheet said 2.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Caladrel's sheet is a drawer from the card catalogue of the Horeck library: every fact is a typed index card behind a coloured guide card, and spells are the old-school spell cards players kept in a shoebox. It refuses the parchment-and-Cinzel fantasy sheet and the dark dashboard of stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Oiled-oak drawer ground (deep brown-black), cool white index stock with a red head rule and pale blue feint lines, typewriter-black ribbon text with red-ribbon emphasis, violet rubber-stamp ink for state, pressboard guide-card tabs in buff, salmon, sage and cadet blue, one school ink per school, brass label-holder for the drawer front. One typewriter face does all the work at strict size steps. Raises: one ink per school, held everywhere (transit map); staggered guide tabs proportional to section length (acetate manual); used slots are stamped, never removed (ticket wallet); a card expands in place while the drawer dims (streaming wall); one face at a strict scale (Emigre specimen).

STORY: He opens the drawer, sees the main record card with every roll he makes, flips to Spells, stamps a slot when he casts, taps a card to read the rule and its DC.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: brass label plate "ECTHËLION, Caladrel — Wiz 7 · Conj." over the record card: AC/touch/flat, HP counter, Init, three saves, BAB/CMB/CMD, spell DCs; the guide-tab rail pinned at the top.

FORM: library card catalogue, position 4 on the ordered list, seed key 767a4968.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
