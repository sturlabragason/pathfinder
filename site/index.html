          Character appearance

          Beneath the unwavering gaze of a full moon, I drew my first breath. Crystalline spires, rising defiantly from the desert's embrace, bore silent witness. Here, in this city of mirrors and secrets, my destiny was etched.

          While others sought solace in fleeting moments of joy, my days were claimed by ancient knowledge and relentless discipline. Tutors, their eyes heavy with the burdens of lost realms, schooled me in the shadows and wonders lurking just beyond the veil of reality. And while children danced under the moonlight's caress, I wrestled with ethereal forces. I felt an innate potential surging within, a promise that one day, my fingers would weave patterns powerful enough to challenge and reshape the very fabric of existence.

          The legacy of the Ecthëlions whispered its weighty expectations, an ever-present echo in my every step. Though born of noble blood, the warmth of my parents' embrace was a tale I knew only from secondhand whispers. Their lives, so intertwined with the affairs of realms and realities beyond, left little room for the son who walked among them.

          In the heart of this maze of reflections, surrounded by the weight of ancient legacies, my resolve was forged. The arcane became more than just gestures and incantations; it was the very essence of my being. To many, my words seemed ensnared in cryptic mysteries, but to me, they were the desperate utterances of one who has peered into the abyss and felt its chilling stare in return.

          As night's mantle enveloped the city, I felt the pull of a deeper, darker purpose. Beneath the very stars that witnessed my birth, a burning resolve kindled. I would harness the boundless powers of the cosmos, pit ancient evil against eldritch horrors, and in the crucible of that apocalyptic clash, eradicate the scourge of malevolence. For within the vast expanse of the universe's enigmas, one truth was irrefutable: I was destined to be its instrument of reckoning.

          Wizard tips

          Act first, shape the fight. Initiative +10 (Improved Initiative + Warrior of Old) means you usually go before the enemy. Spend that turn on control, not damage: a blinded, nauseated or grappled enemy is worth more to the party than a few hit points of damage.

          Black Tentacles. No save and no spell resistance. The tentacles grapple everything in a 20-ft radius with CMB +12 (caster level 7, +4 Str, +1 size), one roll for the whole area each round. Your best opener against a group, but allies in the area get grappled too.

          Glitterdust. Will DC 20 or blinded. Even on a save, invisible creatures are outlined and everyone in it takes −40 on Stealth. Blinded creatures save again at the end of each of their turns, so the party should hit them fast.

          Stinking Cloud. Fort DC 21 or nauseated (only a move action each turn). Creatures that stay inside save again every round. It is fog too: it blocks your line of sight and the party's arrows.

          Create Pit / Spiked Pit. Reflex DC 20 / 21. At caster level 7 the pit is 30 ft deep (falling damage), Climb DC 25 to get out; Spiked Pit adds 2d6 piercing on the fall and 1d6 a round to climbers. Creatures that end their turn next to it save at +2 or slide in, and a bull rush into it allows no save. It needs a horizontal surface.

          Spell resistance. Nearly all your control is conjuration (creation): Black Tentacles, Glitterdust, Web, Stinking Cloud, the pits, Grease, Aqueous Orb, Sleet Storm all say SR no. Against outsiders, fey and casters with SR, use those first.

          Opening moves & control

          Every turn. One standard action (a spell), one move, one swift. Shift is your swift: 15 ft of teleport that provokes no attacks of opportunity, so Shift out of reach, then cast normally.

          Casting defensively. DC 15 + twice the spell level. Concentration +13, +4 from Combat Casting = +17: 1st level always works, 2nd on a 2+, 3rd on a 4+, 4th on a 6+. Shift or a 5-ft step needs no roll, so try those first.

          Summons take a full round. Summon Monster has a 1-round casting time: it takes effect just before your next turn, and the creature then acts on your turn. Being hit while casting forces a concentration check, so summon from behind the line. Summoner's charm adds 3 rounds, Augment Summoning gives +4 Str and Con (+2 to hit and damage, +2 hp per Hit Die).

          The extend rod is free. As a prepared caster you use the lesser extend rod without a longer casting time. It works on spells of 3rd level or lower, so not Black Tentacles.

          Spellcraft +16. Identifying a spell as it is cast is DC 15 + spell level, so spells of 2nd level or lower are automatic and even 7th level needs only a 6. Tell the table what the enemy caster is doing and whether to Dispel it.

          Action economy

          Mage Armor every morning. AC 15 → 19 (touch AC is unchanged), 7 hours. Use the extend rod for 14 hours, or Master of Pentacles for 9.

          Shift, 9/day. They come back every day. Use them for movement and repositioning, not only for escapes.

          Arcane bond, 1/day. Casts any spell in your spellbook without preparing it. Keep it for the spell you didn't expect to need: Dispel Magic, Fly, Rope Trick, Aqueous Orb. Divination and enchantment are your opposed schools and take two slots each to prepare.

          Master of Pentacles, 1/day. Caster level 9 for the duration of one conjuration spell: pits last 10 rounds instead of 8, summons 12 instead of 10 (with Summoner's charm). Use it on the fight's big spell, not on a cantrip.

          Lesser extend rod, 3/day. Best on buffs and long fights: Haste, Fly, Glitterdust, Stinking Cloud, Summon Monster III. Summoner's charm rounds are not doubled.

          Scrolls. Fog Cloud, Knock, See Invisibility, Feather Fall and Mount are utility spells you don't need to prepare. Scribe Scroll and 8 blank scrolls turn spare slots at the end of the day into more.

          Daily resources
          • Buy a miniature shovel (10 gp). Create Pit and Spiked Pit need it as a focus. The spell component pouch only covers components with no listed cost, and Eschew Materials only covers material components, not foci. You have 14 gp.
          • No sleep immunity. Moonkissed traded the elven immunities for +1 on all saves: magic sleep effects work on you, and there is no +2 against enchantments.
          • Your area spells hit friends. Black Tentacles, Stinking Cloud, Glitterdust, Web and Sleet Storm don't pick targets. Tell the party where the area goes before you cast.
          • Your own fog. Obscuring Mist, Fog Cloud and Stinking Cloud block your line of sight as much as the enemy's. Cast them when you don't need to see through them.
          • Web needs anchors. It needs two opposite solid points or it collapses.
          • You are fragile. 40 hp, AC 15, Str 7. Most of your control spells are medium range (170 ft at caster level 7): use it.
          • Watch the load. 19.5 of 23 lb light load. A few more pounds is a medium load: slower and a lower Dex cap on AC.
          • The wand doesn't recharge. 19 charges of detect magic in total. The cantrip is divination, an opposed school, so preparing it costs two of your 4 cantrip slots. Pick which one you spend.
